Construction: covering perfect hash families found by the in-parameter-order (IPO) strategies of M. Wagner, C. J. Colbourn and D. E. Simos, "In-Parameter-Order strategies for covering perfect hash families", Appl. Math. Comput. 421 (2022) 126952, doi:10.1016/j.amc.2022.126952, expanded into covering arrays by the construction of Sherwood, Martirosyan and Colbourn (2006), N = n(v^t - 1) + 1.

Built from: the authors' published data, cphfs.zip at https://srd.sba-research.org/data/cphfipo/cphfs.zip (446 families). These are the seven families of that file that give arrays smaller than the table's best at their size. The rows of each family are unchanged; only the commas are replaced by spaces, and the source file and its sha256 are named in the first line of each family file. Entered and stored with the permission of Dimitris E. Simos, who answered for his group (2026-11-16).

Program: none of ours builds the families; they are the authors' data. The arrays are rebuilt from them by tools/cphf/cphfcheck.c of this repository (`cphfcheck FILE t v expand OUT`). Before this submission each family was checked exactly with cphfcheck in mode fullfast (every set of t columns, 0 uncovered); the exact check runs again on GitHub when the submission reaches main.
