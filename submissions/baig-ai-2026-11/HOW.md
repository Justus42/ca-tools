Construction: both arrays were found by AI discovery systems built in our programme (they are not language models), and both are group constructions of Chateauneuf-Kreher type (M. Chateauneuf and D. L. Kreher, On the state of strength-three covering arrays, J. Combin. Des. 10 (2002) 217-238): the orbits of starting rows under a group acting on the columns and the symbols, together with the constant rows.
- CA(351; 4, 29, 3), found by our discovery system S-4 (preprint: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24473807). The group is C29 x AGL(1, 3). C29 shifts the 29 columns cyclically. AGL(1, 3), the maps x -> a x + b (mod 3) with a in {1, 2} and b in {0, 1, 2}, acts on every entry. The array is the union of the orbits of two starting rows, each of full length 29 x 6 = 174, and the 3 constant rows: 2 x 174 + 3 = 351.
- CA(848; 3, 16, 8), from a structural rule found by our discovery system S-2, made exact and searched with a large language model (preprint: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24447195). The symbols are the elements of GF(8) built from x^3 + x + 1, symbol s being the element whose coefficients are the binary digits of s. The group is C15 x AGL(1, 8). C15 shifts the first 15 columns cyclically and fixes the 16th. AGL(1, 8), the maps x -> a x + b with a != 0, acts on every entry. The array is the orbit of one starting row, of full length 15 x 56 = 840, and the 8 constant rows: 840 + 8 = 848.

Built from: nothing. Both arrays were built from scratch.

Program: the starting rows were found by searches in our AI discovery programme, described in the two preprints above. The search programs are not released while the programme continues. Both arrays are rebuilt exactly from the description above and the starting rows in the files:
- CA(351; 4, 29, 3): the starting rows are rows 2 and 15 of CA_351_4_29_3.txt. Their two orbits under C29 x AGL(1, 3) are disjoint, and together with the constant rows (rows 1, 176 and 351 of the file) they give, as a set, exactly the 351 rows of the file.
- CA(848; 3, 16, 8): the starting row is row 1 of CA_848_3_16_8.txt. Its orbit under C15 x AGL(1, 8) is, as a set, exactly rows 1 to 840 of the file; rows 841 to 848 are the constant rows.
- Both arrays and their SHA-256 are archived on Zenodo, with the preprints, a standalone checker and the verification logs: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24473807 (351 rows) and https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24447195 (848 rows).
- To verify: `cc -O3 -pthread -o ca_verify ca_verify.c`, then `./ca_verify CA_351_4_29_3.txt 4 3` and `./ca_verify CA_848_3_16_8.txt 3 8`. With our checker from the Zenodo records: `python verify_ca.py --t 4 --v 3 CA_351_4_29_3.txt` and `python verify_ca.py --t 3 --v 8 CA_848_3_16_8.txt`.
