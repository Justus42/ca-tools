Construction: Building on group-action constructions for strength-3 covering arrays by Chateauneuf, Colbourn and Kreher (1999, `ck-cck1999`), Chateauneuf and Kreher (2002, `ck-ck2002`), Meagher and Stevens (2005, `group-ms2005`), tabu search by Nurmela (2004, `tabu-n2004`), and most closely Cohen, Colbourn and Ling (2008, Constructions 1-3, `sa-ccl2008`, single-orbit case k <= q + 1): to our knowledge, the specific combination used here extends the PGL(2, q) + binary pair-expansion framework to two deterministic PGL(2, q) orbits with balanced column halving (k <= 2q) and a general (non-self-complementary) weakened partition-dependent binary matrix C (M x k).
- Alphabet and PGL(2, q) block (2 * q(q^2 - 1) rows): symbols represent F_q U {infty} in 0-based encoding {0, ..., q} where q denotes infty (v = q + 1). Columns are partitioned into balanced halves L (|L| = floor(k/2)) and R (|R| = ceil(k/2)), both <= q. Starter r1 puts 0 on L and pairwise distinct nonzero symbols 1..|R| on R (using all q symbols of (F_q \ {0}) U {infty} when |R| = q); starter r2 puts pairwise distinct symbols 0..|L|-1 on L and q (infty) on R. Expanding r1 and r2 under sharply 3-transitive PGL(2, q) deterministically covers all 3-distinct tuples on every column triple, all constant tuples on pure triples (L^3, R^3), all aab patterns on L^2 R triples, and all baa patterns on L R^2 triples.
- Oriented pair expansion of K_v and binary matrix C: all v(v - 1)/2 unordered pairs {u, w} of K_v are oriented via the directed v-cycle (0,1), (1,2), ..., (v-2,v-1), (v-1,0) plus all remaining edges with u < w, so every symbol 0..v-1 occurs at least once in the 0-slot and at least once in the 1-slot. Each row of C maps 0 -> u, 1 -> w across all v(v - 1)/2 oriented pairs. C must cover all 6 non-constant binary triples on L^3 and R^3, {010, 011, 100, 101} on L^2 R, and {001, 010, 101, 110} on L R^2. Two variants of C are used:
  * C_const (`--cover-const`, cc=1, 12 of 13 arrays): additionally requires 000 or 111 on every mixed triple, which covers all constant tuples via the directed cycle and needs 0 appended constant rows (N = 2q(q^2 - 1) + M * v(v - 1)/2).
  * C_plain (`--no-cover-const`, cc=0, used for CA(18070; 3, 35, 20)): omits the 000/111 requirement on mixed triples and appends +v constant rows (N = 2q(q^2 - 1) + M * v(v - 1)/2 + v).
  Because mixed triples omit two non-constant patterns, M is strictly smaller than regular binary covering array sizes (e.g., M = 14 < 17 = CAN(3, 15, 2) [proven exact] at k = 15, and M = 16 < best known upper bound 18 at k = 18, 19).
